Follow these steps every single time. Skipping any step is how wildfires start from "extinguished" campfires.

## Step 1 — Stop feeding the fire

Cease adding wood at least 30 minutes before you plan to extinguish. Let existing fuel burn down to coals.

## Step 2 — Spread the coals

Using a shovel or stick, spread the coals and embers out across the pit. This increases surface area and speeds cooling.

## Step 3 — Add water slowly

- Pour water **gently** over the coals — do not dump it all at once. A sudden steam explosion can scatter hot embers.
- Pour in a circular motion, covering all coals evenly.
- Stir coals with a stick while pouring to expose hidden hot spots.

## Step 4 — Listen and feel

- **Listen** for hissing — as long as steam rises, the fire is not out.
- **Feel** the heat from 30–40 cm above the pit (never touch directly). If you still feel warmth, continue.

## Step 5 — Repeat until cold

Alternate between adding water and stirring until:

- No more steam or smoke appears
- The pit feels cool from 20 cm above
- The ash is wet throughout, not just on the surface

## Step 6 — Final check

Press the back of your hand **cautiously** a few centimetres above the centre of the pit. If there is any warmth, add more water and stir again.

> **Use all your water if necessary.** Carrying heavy water home is far better than causing a wildfire.
