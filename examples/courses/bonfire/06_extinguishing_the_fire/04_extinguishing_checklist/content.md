After your next bonfire, run through this checklist and describe what you did for each item.

## Checklist

For each of the following, write one or two sentences confirming what you did and any observations:

1. **Timing** — did you start extinguishing at least 45 minutes before leaving?
2. **Coal spreading** — how did you spread the coals and what tool did you use?
3. **Water application** — how much water did you use and how many rounds of pouring and stirring?
4. **Final temperature check** — was the pit cold to the back of your hand?
5. **Site restoration** — what did you do to restore the site?
6. **Waste** — what did you carry out?

Be honest about anything you would do differently next time.
