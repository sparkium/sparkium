## What to record

- **GPS coordinates** (latitude / longitude) or a What3Words address
- **Nearest road or trail access point** — important for emergency services
- **Distance to water** — estimate how long it takes to fetch water on foot

- Has open ground (no forest canopy directly above)
- Is within 200 m of a stream, lake or other water source
