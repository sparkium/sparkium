A map is your best friend when scouting a bonfire location from home before you even set foot outdoors.

## Maps to check

- **Swiss National Map (swisstopo)** — shows forest boundaries, water sources, and terrain. Available at [map.geo.admin.ch](https://map.geo.admin.ch).
- **Cantonal fire-ban layers** — some cantons publish active fire-ban zones as map overlays.
- **Land-use zones** — identify private land, nature reserves, and protected areas where fires are forbidden.

## Choose a place

Look for locations that meet all of the following:

1. **Open ground** — at least 5 metres from any tree, shrub or dry grass.
2. **Near a water source** — a stream or lake within reasonable distance is ideal.
3. **Accessible** — you need to be able to carry water and tools to the site.
4. **Not in a designated fire-ban zone** — cross-check with the current day's fire-ban status.

## Plan for alternatives

Always identify two or three candidate spots. Conditions on the ground (dry vegetation, other groups, wind direction) may rule out your first choice. Having a backup means you never have to compromise on safety.

> **Tip:** Screenshot your chosen spot and the nearest water source before going offline.
