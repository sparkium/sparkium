Hello and welcome to this course! Let's start with a quick introduction to what you can expect from this course.

We love being outdoors, next to a sparkling campfire with friends, cooking food and sharing stories. Especially here in Switzerland we are quite liberal when it comes to building a bonfire. However, it's better for everyone if you know what you are doing, so it hopefully stays this way and we can all enjoy of a crackling fire in the future.

Learn how to pack the essentials for a bonfire and make the most out of your outdoor experience. In this course, you will learn how to prepare for a bonfire, what materials you need, and how to set up a safe and enjoyable fire. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced camper, this course will provide you with valuable tips and tricks to enhance your bonfire experience. So grab your marshmallows and let's get started!
