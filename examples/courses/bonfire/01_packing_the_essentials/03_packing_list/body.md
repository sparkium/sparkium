Before heading out for a bonfire, pack everything you need so nothing is forgotten.

## Fire-Starting Kit

- **Lighter or waterproof matches** — bring at least two independent ignition sources
- **Fire starter sticks or cubes** — helpful in damp conditions
- **Dry tinder** — cotton balls with petroleum jelly, or commercial tinder

## Tools

- **Folding saw or hatchet** — for processing wood on site
- **Shovel or small trowel** — for clearing the fire pit and burying embers
- **Metal tongs or fire poker** — to adjust burning logs safely
- **Heat-resistant gloves** — for handling hot equipment

## Safety Essentials

- **Water (at least 10 litres)** — always have enough to fully extinguish the fire
- **First aid kit** — with burn treatment supplies
- **Phone with offline maps** — in case of emergencies

## Comfort & Leave-No-Trace

- **Headlamp or torch** — bonfires often run past dark
- **Rubbish bag** — take all waste home with you
- **Seating** — a folding mat or log seat keeps you comfortable

> **Tip:** Lay everything out at home the evening before. Forgetting water or a shovel is the most common — and most dangerous — mistake.
