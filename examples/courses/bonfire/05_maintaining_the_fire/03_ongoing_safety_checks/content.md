Once the fire is burning, your job is not done. Regular checks keep small problems from becoming emergencies.

## Check every 15–20 minutes

### 1. Perimeter check
Walk around the fire and inspect the ground within a 2-metre radius. Look for:
- Smouldering leaves or grass that a spark may have ignited
- Dry material that has been blown closer by wind

### 2. Wind direction
Note whether the wind has shifted. Adjust seating positions and check whether the downwind area is still clear of combustibles.

### 3. Water supply
Confirm your water containers are still within reach and have not been accidentally moved or knocked over.

### 4. Fire size
Is the fire still within the pit? Are flames within the safe height range? If not, stop feeding and let it reduce.

### 5. Group awareness
Check that no children or animals are near the fire. Confirm everyone in the group knows where the water is and how to use it.

## When to extinguish immediately

Stop the session and begin extinguishing if any of the following occur:

- Sparks are landing beyond the cleared zone and smouldering
- Wind increases significantly and cannot be predicted to calm
- A participant shows signs of smoke inhalation or burn injury
- The fire begins to spread outside the pit

> **Assign a fire watcher** — especially if children are present. One person should stay near the fire at all times and not be distracted by phones or conversation.
