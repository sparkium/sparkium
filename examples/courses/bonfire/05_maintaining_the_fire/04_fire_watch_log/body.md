During your next bonfire session, keep a simple log of your safety checks. This builds the habit of active monitoring.

## What to record

For each check you perform (aim for at least 3 during a session), write:

1. **Time** — approximately how many minutes into the session
2. **Perimeter** — did you spot any smouldering material outside the pit? What did you do?
3. **Wind** — had direction or strength changed since lighting?
4. **Fire size** — was it within the safe range? Did you need to adjust?
5. **Water** — was it still accessible and adequate?

Write a few sentences per check and a short overall reflection at the end about what you noticed and what you would improve next time.
