With your pit prepared and your wood stacked, you are ready to light. Follow these steps carefully.

## Step 1 — Prepare your tinder bundle

Shape your tinder into a loose "bird's nest" — a cupped handful. Loose packing is critical: fire needs air to breathe.

## Step 2 — Place the tinder

Set the tinder bundle in the centre of your teepee structure. If using a fire-starter cube, place it at the base of the teepee.

## Step 3 — Ignite

- Use a lighter or match held **at the base** of the tinder, not the top. Heat rises.
- Light on the upwind side so the flame is pushed into the structure, not away from it.
- Apply flame steadily for 3–5 seconds before moving.

## Step 4 — Nurture the flame

- Gently blow at the base of the flame with long, slow breaths to increase airflow.
- Add slightly larger kindling once the tinder is burning well.
- **Do not rush.** Adding fuel logs too early smothers the fire.

## Step 5 — Transition to fuel logs

Once your kindling is burning steadily and you have a visible bed of small coals (typically 5–10 minutes), begin adding fuel logs — one at a time, with space between them for airflow.

## Common mistakes

- **Smothering** — too much wood too soon. Start small.
- **Wind problems** — shield the flame during ignition with your body.
- **Damp tinder** — always have a backup supply stored dry in your pack.
