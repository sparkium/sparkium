A safe fire pit is the foundation of a safe fire. Never skip this step, even at a site you have used before.

## Step 1 — Choose open ground

- At least 5 metres from trees, shrubs, tents, and dry grass
- Avoid areas with low-hanging branches overhead
- Avoid slopes — embers roll downhill

## Step 2 — Clear the area

1. Remove all dry leaves, pine needles, and organic debris from a 1-metre radius around the pit.
2. Dig or scrape a shallow depression (10–15 cm deep) in mineral soil. Exposed mineral soil does not catch fire.
3. If using an existing fire ring, clear any accumulated ash and debris before building your fire.

## Step 3 — Set up your water supply

- Place your water containers **within arm's reach** before you light anything.
- 10 litres is a minimum; 20 litres is better for a large fire.

## Step 4 — Check wind direction

- Position yourself and your seated guests **upwind** of the fire.
- Note which direction sparks will travel and ensure there is no combustible material downwind.

> **Never** build a fire directly on grass, leaf litter, or peat. Underground root systems can smoulder for days and reignite spontaneously.
