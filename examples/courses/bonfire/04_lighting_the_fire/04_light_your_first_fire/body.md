It's time to put theory into practice. Go through the full process — from preparing your pit to having a stable, burning fire — and reflect on the experience.

## What to write

Describe your experience in detail:

1. **Location and conditions** — where did you light the fire? What was the weather and fire-danger level?
2. **Preparation** — how did you prepare the pit and what structure did you choose?
3. **Ignition** — how many attempts did it take? What worked, what didn't?
4. **Lessons learned** — what would you do differently next time?

Your response should be detailed enough that someone else could learn from your experience.
