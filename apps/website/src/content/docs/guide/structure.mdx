**In Sparkium, the organization of your course content happens through the file system.** The structuring should feel natural and intuitive, like when you organize your personal files on your computer. This approach allows you to easily manage and navigate everything without needing to learn a complex system.

## Hierarchy

The hierarchy of your content is determined by the folder structure. Each folder represents a level of organization, and the files within those folders hold the actual content.

The **root folder** is the starting point. This folder represents your entire **course**. Inside the root folder, you can create subfolders to further structure your course. For example, you might have a folder for each **module**, and inside each module, you may have one or more **materials** for every lesson or topic.

## What's next

You've now seen how the file system maps to your course. Each level in this hierarchy has its own role, and the following sections break them down one by one:

- **[Course](/course/)** -- the root folder that represents your entire course.
- **[Module](/module/)** -- a folder grouping related materials into one unit.
- **[Material](/material/)** -- the materials used in each module.
